# Two-week senior iOS preparation process

## Goal

Improve interview confidence and build a focused job pipeline. An offer within two weeks is a target, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Daily routine

1. Join the agreed daily interview time; reschedule around real interviews or work.
2. Complete the interview block, taking one question at a time.
3. Spend 10–15 additional minutes reviewing answers and practicing corrected explanations.
4. Choose at most three focused practice tasks.
5. Research suitable roles and prepare targeted applications alongside interview practice.

## Duration and topics

| Completed session | Interview minutes | Focus |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 15 | Introduction, project ownership, Swift baseline |
| 2–4 | 30 | Swift, ARC, UIKit/SwiftUI, state, concurrency |
| 5–7 | 45 | DSA in Swift, architecture, testing, mixed mock |
| 8–10 | 60 | Mobile system design, DSA, production debugging |
| 11–12 | 75 | JD-specific technical, coding, leadership mocks |
| 13–14 | 90 | Full interview simulations and reassessment |

Advance by completed sessions rather than elapsed dates. Repeat a duration when helpful. Pause on request. A missed day does not automatically raise difficulty.

## JD-based preparation

The app ranks JD competencies and extracts relevant résumé evidence. This keyword-based mapping is a starting point, not a full assessment of fit. Map the JD's requirements to evidence from the candidate's experience. Separate demonstrated strengths from unknowns. Select relevant technical, coding, design, and behavioral questions. Never invent experience or achievements. Revisit earlier misconceptions across sessions.

## Feedback record

For each session, record the question, the actual answer, strengths, misconceptions, a corrected explanation, a retry, and up to three next actions. Assess technical accuracy, reasoning, communication and coding independently. Confidence is optional self-report. Do not assign scores to unobserved performance.

## Job hunt

Confirm geography, remote preference, work authorization where relevant, availability and compensation preferences. Verify current employer links before shortlisting roles. Tailor truthful résumé bullets to relevant JDs. Track employer, role, URL, match evidence, application stage and next action. Obtain explicit authorization before sending applications or outreach.

## Current scheduled routine

The user's requested daily slot is 2 PM India time, adjustable around commitments. Reminders are managed outside this repository in the originating chat. Reminder invitations do not start voice calls automatically.
